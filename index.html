Travel to Scotland.

Experience the Magic of Scotland.

A Journey Through Five Historic Cities:

  1. Edinburgh - The Capital of Enchantment

    What to see: Explore the majestic Edinburgh Castle, perched atop an extinct volcano, offering panoramic city views. Wander down the historic Royal Mile, a cobbled street connecting the castle to the Palace of Holyroodhouse. Climb Arthur's Seat, an ancient volcano, for a stunning sunrise or sunset. Visit the Palace of Holyroodhouse, the King's official residence in Scotland. What to Do: Dive into the city's hidden closes and wynds. Enjoy a traditional Scottish ceilidh (folk dance). Take a ghost tour to discover Edinburgh's spooky past. Browse the shops on Princes Street and enjoy the stunning views of the castle. What Must-Try Food: Haggis (often served with neeps and tatties - turnips and potatoes), shortbread, and a dram of fine Scotch whisky.

  2. Glasgow - The Creative Hub.

    What to See: Admire the stunning Victorian architecture of the Glasgow City Chambers. Immerse yourself in art and history at the Kelvingrove Art Gallery and Museum. Discover the works of Charles Rennie Mackintosh at the Glasgow School of Art. What to Do: Explore the vibrant street art scene. Catch a live music show in one of the city's many famous venues. Shop 'til you drop in the city center, known as a shopping paradise. Must-Try Food: Bangers and Mash (sausages and mashed potatoes), a rich steak pie, and a "wee" pint in a traditional pub.

  3. Stirling - The Gateway to the Highlands.

    ​What to See: Stand where history was made at Stirling Castle, a grand fortress that played a key role in Scotland's past. Visit the National Wallace Monument, honoring the Scottish hero William Wallace. Explore the medieval Old Town. What to Do: Learn about the battles of Stirling Bridge and Bannockburn. Walk along the historic city walls. Take in the dramatic views of the Ochil Hills. Must-Try Food: A hearty bowl of Cullen Skink (smoked haddock soup) and fresh, local produce from one of the city's farmers' markets.

  4. Inverness - The Highland Capital.

    What to See: Search for the legendary Loch Ness Monster on a boat tour. Explore the beautiful Inverness Cathedral. Walk along the picturesque banks of the River Ness. Visit the nearby Culloden Battlefield, a site of immense historical significance. ​What to Do: Use Inverness as your base to explore the stunning Scottish Highlands. Go hiking, cycling, or fishing. Enjoy a traditional Highland evening with local music and storytelling. ​Must-Try Food: Freshly caught salmon, venison, and a pint of local craft beer.

  5. Aberdeen - The Granite City.

    ​What to See: Marvel at the shimmering granite buildings of Aberdeen, which sparkle in the sunlight. Stroll along the beautiful sandy beach. Visit the historic Old Aberdeen, home to the University of Aberdeen's stunning King's College. ​What to Do: Discover the city's maritime history at the Aberdeen Maritime Museum. Explore the beautiful Duthie Park Winter Gardens. Take a day trip to the stunning coastline and charming fishing villages. ​Must-Try Food: Aberdeen Angus beef, renowned for its quality, and fresh seafood from the North Sea.
